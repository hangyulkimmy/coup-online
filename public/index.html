    How to Play COUP

    The Goal

    Be the last player with any influence left. Your influence is your two face-down character cards — lose both and you're out of the game.

    Setup & Your Turn

    • Everyone starts with 2 coins and 2 influence (hidden character cards).
    • On your turn you take one action (below), then play passes on.
    • If you ever start a turn with 10+ coins, you must Coup.
    • You can bluff — claim any character whether or not you actually hold it. The risk is being challenged.

    Basic Actions (always available, no character needed)

    • Income — Take 1 coin. Totally safe, can't be stopped.
    • Foreign Aid — Take 2 coins. Can be blocked by a Duke.
    • Coup — Pay 7 coins and force a player to lose an influence. Cannot be blocked or challenged.

    Character Actions (claim the character — you may be bluffing)

    DukeTax: take 3 coins. Blocks Foreign Aid.
    AssassinPay 3 coins to make a player lose an influence.
    CaptainSteal 2 coins from a player. Blocks stealing.
    AmbassadorExchange: draw 2 from the deck, keep the best, return 2. Blocks stealing.
    ContessaBlocks assassination.

    Challenges

    Whenever someone claims a character (for an action or a block), any other player may Challenge it:

    • If the claimer really has the card, they prove it — the challenger loses an influence, and the claimer shuffles that card back and draws a new one.
    • If they were bluffing, the claimer loses an influence and their action fails.

    Blocks

    Some actions can be stopped by claiming a blocking character — Duke blocks Foreign Aid, Contessa blocks assassination, Captain/Ambassador block stealing. A block can itself be challenged (maybe the blocker is bluffing!).

    Losing Influence & Winning

    When you lose influence, you reveal one of your cards (your choice) face-up for good. At 0 influence you're eliminated. The last player standing wins. 🏆

    Optional Modes

    The host can toggle these in the lobby before starting.

    • Inquisitor mode — Replaces the Ambassador. Exchange 1 card (not 2), blocks stealing, and adds Interrogate: force a player to secretly show you one card, then choose whether to make them swap it.
    • Turn timer — If a player stalls, the game auto-acts for them (Income on your turn, Pass on prompts) so nobody freezes the game. Pick 20s–2m or a custom length.
